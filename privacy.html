Privacy

Sextant Studio · Effective November 1, 2026

The object transmits nothing. A Sextant has no account, no telemetry, and no connection to us or anyone else.

This site

It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from anyone else. When you send the fitting request, it goes to our web host, Netlify, which delivers it to us.

What we keep

Your name, your email, and your phone number if you give one; what you tell us in the form and at the fitting; your delivery address, configuration and number; and your first logbook line, only if you choose to share it.

What we do with it

We use it to build, deliver and support your Sextant, and to write to you about it. We don't sell it. We share it only with those who need it to do their part: the carrier, the power station's maker, which ships it to you directly, and our payment processor.

How long

For as long as you own a Sextant, so the ledger and the warranty work, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choice

Ask us what we hold, to correct it, or to delete it, at any time: commissions@madebysextant.com.

Changes

If this changes, the date above changes, and owners hear from us first.
