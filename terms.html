Terms of commission

  1. What you're commissioning

    A numbered Sextant, built to the configuration in your proposal. Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax is added where it applies.

  2. The price

    The first edition, No. 001 to No. 010, is $13,500. From No. 011 the price is $15,000. The price in your proposal is the price you pay.

    Half the price reserves your number. It is fully refundable for 72 hours. After that we order components, and the deposit is non-refundable except as these terms say.

  4. The balance

    Due when your Sextant is finished, before it ships, by card, wire or ACH, at the same price.

  5. Build time

    Your proposal gives a ship date. If it will slip by more than two weeks, we tell you, and you may cancel for a full refund of everything you've paid.

    If a component is discontinued during your build, we substitute one of equal or better specification and tell you first.

  7. If we cancel

    If we can't complete your commission for any reason, we refund everything you've paid.

  8. Delivery

    Within the United States only. In San Diego, Orange, Riverside and Los Angeles counties, the maker delivers by hand. Elsewhere, insured ground. The power station ships separately, from its maker. Risk passes to you on delivery.

  9. Arrival

    If anything arrives damaged or doesn't work, tell us within 14 days of delivery and we repair or replace it at our cost.

  10. Returns

    Each Sextant is engraved and configured for its owner, so we don't accept change-of-mind returns. This doesn't affect the arrival promise above or the warranty.

  11. Warranty

    For two years from delivery, we repair or replace at our cost any fault in our work: the build, the archive, the foam and felt, the plate, and the fit of the trays. Each component (the laptop, the case, the power station, the purifier and the radios) carries its own maker's warranty, and we help you use it. The warranty doesn't cover misuse, accidents, or sealed components that have been opened.

    Sextant holds reference material and language models that answer from it. Answers cite their source and can be wrong. It isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. The case liner's certification is its fabric manufacturer's; the assembled case hasn't been independently tested. Water purification performance is as stated by the purifier's manufacturer.

  14. Software

    Sextant doesn't update itself; it is designed to work as delivered. An archive refresh is offered every year and is never required.

    We deliver within the United States. If you take your Sextant abroad, compliance with export and import rules, including for encryption, radios and drones, is your responsibility. The radios use 915 MHz, which is license-free in the United States and isn't everywhere else.
